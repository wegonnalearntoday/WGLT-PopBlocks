How to Play

Tap a connected group of two or more same-color blocks to pop them. Blocks above fall down, and empty columns slide left. Every few successful pops, the game reveals a fact or a quick quiz tied to the current WGLT subject.

Correct quiz answer: bonus points and extra block clears.
Wrong quiz answer: no bonus this round, but you can keep learning and keep playing.
